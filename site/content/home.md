**loremfile.dev** — sample files you can hotlink.

Free, CC0, stable forever. PDFs, images, audio, video, office documents, data files, text in
many encodings and exact-size binary blobs. Open CORS, byte ranges and a SHA-256 manifest.
No ads, no signup, no keys.

```bash
curl -O https://loremfile.dev/pdf/a4-3pages.pdf
```
